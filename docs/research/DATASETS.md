# VOXIQ Research & Speech Datasets Evaluation

This document outlines standard speech corpora evaluated for acoustic modeling, speech recognition benchmarking, and disfluency research.

| Dataset | Modality | Primary Application | License & Restrictions | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mozilla Common Voice** | Multilingual Speech Audio | Acoustic diversity & STT evaluation | CC0 Public Domain | Broad demographic diversity; useful for multi-speaker pacing evaluation. |
| **AMI Meeting Corpus** | Multi-speaker Audio & Video | Turn-taking & Meeting Diarization | CC BY 4.0 | Real conversational interruptions, overlaps, and multi-speaker pauses. |
| **LibriSpeech ASR** | Clean / Other Read Speech | Acoustic preprocessing baseline | CC BY 4.0 | 1,000 hours of 16kHz audio; ideal for baseline WER and SNR benchmarking. |
| **Switchboard Corpus** | Conversational Telephone Speech | Disfluency & Filler word research | LDC Restricted / Academic | Standard reference corpus for speech disfluency ('um', 'uh', restarts). |
